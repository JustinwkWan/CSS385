The game I am drawn to is called keep talking and no one explodes. This game spoke to me because of the time pressure, collaboration, and quick wittedness that the players have to use in order to win. I feel like games are about getting people together and having a fun time and is a great plus that you need to use your brain to win! With this in mind, I am thinking about making a board-game like project where you have to complete puzzles in order to win.
