Game Design Principles (different than ones talked about in class):
1 Easy to Learn, Hard to Master: Games such as geometry dash start very simple with just clicking the screen to jump over blocks and progressively get harder needing precise timing to finish the level. 
Pros: Lets players get up to their potential on the skills the game requires to master
Cons: May leave out more casual players who are not as competitive
Summary: I would use this game principle in my game by letting players complete easy puzzles, eventually adding more steps to each to add a layer of difficultly and after add qualitatively harder puzzles to the game to keep it interesting. I believe using this Easy to Learn, Hard to Master is what is important for a games longevity because the thing about games is how there is something to achieve/something new to explore and doing it through game difficulty is something I have experienced a lot in my time playing.
2 Strategic depth: Teamfight tactics has many interactions that a player needs to learn and all these compounding areas 
Pro: It makes the game more replayable as you are able to use what you learned in your past games into the new ones and are always finding new things to be able to do/learn.
Cons: Some of these games have high learning curves and would be hard to develop due to all the interactions/types of game styles the developers have to do. 
Summary: I believe that this depth makes for interesting game design however I belive it will be hard to pull of especially as a first time game developer because it requires constant changes to the game to input new challenges which will be harder for indie developers vs a large team.
3 Constraints: Clash Royale you have a regular amount of elixir and can only have up to 10 while you play. 
Pros: It forces you to understand how to best use your resources.
Cons: May limit the creativity the players may have and possibilities resulting in some players not liking the game as much. 
Summary: I think constraints are a fantastic way to get players to get players to best use their resources because it forces them to think of all the different options they could do and optimize. It can result in even more creative plays due to all the thinking they have to do to make a new play/mechanic work!
